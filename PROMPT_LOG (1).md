# Prompt Log — Header Tagline

## 1. My Prompt

> Context: I'm a second-year Computer Information Systems student building a personal
> portfolio website for a university web technologies assignment. My header currently
> just says "Computer Information Systems student, Year 2," which feels flat.
> Goal: Write a short tagline (one sentence, under 12 words) to go under my name in the
> page header.
> Constraints: Friendly and approachable tone, not overly formal or corporate. Avoid
> generic buzzwords like "passionate" or "dynamic." Should hint at both the technical
> and design/UX side of CIS work.
> Format: Return 3 tagline options as a plain list, no explanation.

## 2. AI's Raw Output

> 1. Building things people actually enjoy using — one line of code at a time.
> 2. CIS student who likes making software both work well and feel good to use.
> 3. Turning ideas into interfaces, and interfaces into something people trust.

## 3. My Edited Final Version

> CIS student who likes making software work well and feel good to use.

## 4. Reflection

<!-- TODO: adjust this if your own edits differed -->
I picked option 2 because it felt the most natural and least like marketing copy, but I
dropped the word "making" repetition by trimming it to "who likes making software work
well" — cleaner rhythm when read aloud. I didn't go with option 1 or 3 because they
leaned a bit too poetic/vague for a plain student profile page. The final version still
does what I wanted: it's friendly, short, and hints at caring about both function and
usability without sounding like a corporate slogan.
